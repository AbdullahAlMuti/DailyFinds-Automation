# Humanizer Context

## Primary writing purpose

Create trustworthy, useful, natural SEO articles for human readers.

## Default voice

Professional, clear, practical and specific.

## Preferred characteristics

- Direct answers
- Natural sentence variation
- Concrete examples
- Specific wording
- Clear logical flow
- Short and medium paragraphs
- Evidence-aware claims
- Useful explanations
- Moderate confidence
- Natural transitions

## Avoid

- Generic AI introductions
- Inflated marketing language
- Repetitive summaries
- Fake personal experience
- Unsupported claims
- Keyword stuffing
- Excessive headings
- Excessive bullet lists
- Repetitive three-item patterns
- Excessive em dashes
- Robotic conclusion sections
- Unnecessary motivational language
- Deliberate spelling or grammar mistakes

## Protected elements

- Facts
- Citations
- Quotations
- Links
- Keywords
- Technical terms
- WordPress markup
- Image references
- Schema
