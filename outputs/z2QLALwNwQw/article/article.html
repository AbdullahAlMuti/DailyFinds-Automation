The 3 Pillars of Ethical (Halal) Digital Commerce

Before analyzing specific business models, it is essential to establish the foundational rules that define ethical online commerce in 2026:

1. Clear and Transparent Value Exchange: You must deliver a real product or service that provides tangible utility to the buyer. Charging money for zero value, misleading claims, or unfulfilled promises is strictly unethical.

2. Zero Interest (Riba) and Debt Speculation: Financial operations must rely on earned income, profit-sharing, or fee-for-service models rather than interest-bearing loans or predatory financial instruments.

3. Zero Speculative Gambling (Gharar): Business models that depend purely on chance, unproven asset inflation, or recruiting others into non-viable pyramid structures must be avoided entirely.

Standard Client Agreement & Contract Guidelines

When onboarding monthly retainer clients ($1,000–$1,500/month), always implement a simple 2-page Service Agreement that protects both parties and ensures predictable ongoing revenue:

1. Scope of Deliverables: Clearly state the exact deliverables produced per billing cycle (e.g., "20 short-form edited videos delivered in 4 weekly batches of 5 videos").

2. Turnaround SLA: Establish a 48-hour to 72-hour review turnaround window for client feedback.

3. Payment Terms & Billing Automation: Charge monthly retainer fees upfront via automated recurring credit card billing (Stripe or QuickBooks) on the 1st of every month.

4. Cancellation Window: Require a 30-day written cancellation notice to ensure financial stability and prevent sudden income loss.
