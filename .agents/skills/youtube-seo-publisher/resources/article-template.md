# Article Template & Structure Rules

All generated articles must strictly follow this structure for DailyFindz:

## 1. Title (H1)
- Exactly one `# Title` per article.
- Target long-tail keyword must appear naturally near the start.
- Engaging, helpful, and non-clickbait.

## 2. Introduction & Quick Verdict
- **Hook / Context**: Why this topic matters to the reader.
- **Direct Answer**: Address the main search intent in the first 2-3 paragraphs.
- **Key Takeaways Box**: Bullets summarizing core insights.

## 3. Core Content Sections (H2 & H3)
- Use descriptive H2 headings logically ordered by topic sequence.
- Use H3 sub-headings for breakdown where appropriate.
- Keep paragraphs short (2-4 sentences max).
- Add comparison tables or step-by-step numbered lists where useful for clarity.

## 4. Key Lessons & Tradeoffs
- Highlight mistakes to avoid or unexpected findings.
- Maintain honest, transparent "friendly expert" tone.

## 5. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)
- 2-4 relevant questions as H3 headings.
- Concise, clear answers suitable for FAQ schema.

## 6. Conclusion & Related Reading
- Summary of next steps or final recommendations.
- Internal link to relevant DailyFindz category or related guide.

## Mandatory DailyFindz Formatting Rules:
- **No Fabricated First-Person**: If based on a 3rd-party video, present as an objective synthesis guide. Do not write "I bought this last week" unless it is creator Muti's own video.
- **No Affiliate Links**: Monetization is currently paused. Do not add product affiliate links or commercial disclosures.
- **Single H1**: Exactly 1 top-level heading.
