# Research & Verification Rules

Every article published on DailyFindz must be grounded in verified facts:

1. **Fact vs. Opinion Separation**:
   - Extract material claims from transcript (prices, specifications, dimensions, scientific claims, dates).
   - Classify each claim: Fact, Opinion, Prediction, or Anecdote.

2. **Web Source Verification**:
   - For every factual claim, perform current web searches to verify accuracy.
   - Record claim details in `claim-check.md` and source citations in `sources.json`.
   - Prefer primary sources (official documentation, manufacturer specs, government agencies, peer-reviewed research) over secondary blogs.

3. **No Hallucinated Citations**:
   - Do NOT invent study results, statistics, URLs, quotes, or authors.
   - If a claim cannot be verified via reputable sources, either omit it or explicitly state that it is an unverified video speaker claim.

4. **Third-Party Video Attribution**:
   - Give proper credit to the original video channel/speaker when referring to specific techniques or demonstrations presented in the video.
