# DailyFindz Brand & Editorial Identity Guidelines

> [!IMPORTANT]
> This resource defines the exact brand standards, visual identity, color palette, and voice for all content published to **DailyFindz.com**.

---

## 1. Brand Identity & Positioning
- **Domain**: `https://dailyfindz.com`
- **Author**: Muti (Founder & Site Administrator)
- **Positioning**: An honest, practical, reader-first publication covering four primary categories:
  - **Home & Kitchen** (Category ID: 3)
  - **Electronics** (Category ID: 4)
  - **Beauty** (Category ID: 5)
  - **Gadgets** (Category ID: 6)

---

## 2. Voice & Tone
- **Tone**: Honest, practical, "friendly expert" — advice from a knowledgeable friend, never a hype-driven sales page.
- **Narrative Rule**:
  - Write in **first person** ONLY when the content is creator Muti's own personal experience.
  - If processing third-party video content, write an **original synthesis guide** citing sources, presenting public knowledge, and offering practical analysis without fake first-person claims ("I bought this and tested it").
- **Transparency**: Admit mistakes and tradeoffs. Imperfection reads as trustworthy.

---

## 3. Visual Identity & Color Palette
- **Primary Brand Blue**: `#1D4ED8` (Hover: `#1E40AF`)
- **Deal Amber Accent**: `#F59E0B`
- **Navy Ink**: `#0F172A` (Footer: `#021130`)
- **Body Copy**: `#111827`, Secondary/Muted: `#64748B`
- **Page Background**: `#F1F5F9` (Mist), Cards: `#FFFFFF` with `#E2E8F0` borders
- **Typography**: Native system font stack (no webfont downloads for maximum speed).

---

## 4. Hard Guardrails & Monetization Pause
- **Affiliate Links**: **STRICTLY PAUSED / DISABLED** sitewide pending AdSense approval. Do NOT add affiliate links, product affiliate buttons, or affiliate disclosures to any post.
- **Comments**: Set to `closed` sitewide.
- **Images**: Max 1200px wide, JPEG ~75-80% or WEBP, progressive encoding, target <150KB per image. Descriptive hyphenated filename and descriptive alt text required for every image.
